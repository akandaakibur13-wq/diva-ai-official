Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026 Applies to: this website & the DIVA AI Android app

DIVA AI (“we”, “us”) builds a personal AI assistant for Android and this official website. This policy explains, in plain language, what information is involved when you use them and what choices you have.

1. This website

This site is a static website. It has no login system, no comment forms and no databases. Specifically:

  • We do not ask you to create an account, and there is no way to sign in.
  • The contact form does not send data to a server. It simply opens your own email app with a pre-written message.
  • We do not set advertising or tracking cookies.
  • Fonts, scripts and images are served from this site itself — no third-party tracking scripts are loaded.

Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) for security and reliability. Those logs are controlled by the host, not by us.

2. The DIVA AI Android app

What the app needs

  • Internet access — to generate AI replies.
  • Microphone — only while you are actively using a voice feature. It does not listen in the background.
  • Audio playback — to speak replies using text-to-speech.

What the app does not do

  • It does not read your contacts, photos, files, calendar or location.
  • It does not run in the background to record audio.
  • It does not sell or rent personal information to anyone.

3. Your conversations

When you send a message or speak a command, the text is transmitted so that a reply can be generated. Conversations may be stored locally on your device so you can pick up where you left off. You can clear this at any time from within the app, and uninstalling the app removes locally stored data.

Please avoid sharing sensitive personal information — such as passwords, bank details or government ID numbers — with any AI assistant, including DIVA.

4. Children

DIVA AI is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help you remove it.

5. Your choices

  • Revoke microphone access at any time in Android Settings → Apps → DIVA AI → Permissions.
  • Clear conversation history inside the app.
  • Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data.

6. Downloads & updates

The APK available on this site is the official build distributed by us. We recommend downloading it only from this website. If you obtain DIVA AI from another source, we cannot vouch for its contents.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the “last updated” date at the top of this page will change too. Significant updates will be mentioned in the release notes on the Download page.

8. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email us at support@divaai.app or use the Contact page.

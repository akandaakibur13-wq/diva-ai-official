The idea

Built for how people
actually talk

Most assistants feel like a form you fill in. DIVA was designed the other way around: a warm personality, a real voice, and the ability to follow a conversation in the language you're comfortable in — even when you mix two together.

She can help with studying, writing, planning, quick answers or just keeping you company. Ask in Hindi, in English, or in the Hinglish you use with friends. She'll answer the same way.

Conversation first

Replies are written the way a person would say them out loud — not like a search result.

A voice, not a robot

A natural female voice with real intonation, so listening to her doesn't feel like a chore.

Privacy by default

No contacts, no gallery, no location. Just the permissions needed to chat and to listen when you ask.

Light and fast

Under 25 MB, tuned for budget phones and older Android versions. No bloat, no ads.
